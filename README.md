# auditory-neurons-cochlear-implant-stimulus-model
Implementation of a novel phenomenological model (boulet et al) of auditory neurons population response to complex electrical stimuli expected in cochlear implants (CI), with the goal of improving CI coding strategies, and thus the quality of sound perception in patients.

Research performed for my Master thesis "Neural modelling of complex pulse train stimulation to improve cochlear implant coding strategies" at the ETH and Unispital Zurich under supervision of Prof. Dillier and Dr. Tabibi at Laboratory for Experimental Audiology, Unispital Zurich.
